FTR Academy

India is building the fabs. We build the workforce that builds them.

A national training network that certifies the skilled trades who build chip plants and data centres, taught by Irish and European master trainers inside India’s existing government training institutes.

01 The gap Why this exists

Nobody is training the people who build them.

Every chip plant and data centre is a construction project before it is a factory. National skilling programmes aim at chip design and running fabs. Almost nothing is aimed at the specialist trades who physically build, install and Proving a finished facility actually works as designed before it is handed over. the facility.

FTR Academy fills that gap: a training network taught by Irish and European master trainers, with certification aligned to national standards, delivered through the ITIs that already exist in every state.

02 The model One hub, many spokes

One hub. Many spokes. One standard.

Training is delivered through institutes that already exist, upgraded to one specification, so there is no wait for new buildings.

  1. 01 The hub

    International Skills Academy

    About 1,50,000 sq ft, with India’s first full-scale cleanroom construction practice bay and 24 practical training bays, replicating live fab and data-hall conditions.

  2. 02 The spokes

    Government ITI Centres of Excellence

    Existing Government training institutes upgraded to one uniform specification: orbital welding, high- and low-voltage rigs, cooling rigs and data-hall assemblies.

  3. 03 The cascade

    Certified local trainers

    Serving ITI instructors are certified by resident Irish master trainers over an 8-week programme, so the capability stays with the State permanently.

Recognised everywhere

Certification is aligned to NSQF and NCVET, so a certificate earned at any centre is valid on a live project anywhere in India, and abroad.

Learning by doing

Seventy per cent practical, in bays that replicate live conditions.

Skill is proven on physical evidence: weld logs, pressure-test packs and termination records, the same records a live project runs on.

05 The faculty Taught, not bought

This capability can’t be bought. It has to be taught.

Subsidy can pay for the building and the tools. It cannot conjure experienced fab operators or cleanroom contractors. Those take decades to build, or they are transferred deliberately.

Intel has invested over €30 billion in Ireland since 1989, and a further €5 billion Leixlip expansion needs roughly 2,000 specialist tradespeople on its own. That is the heritage our trainers come from.

  • Irish and European master trainers with 30+ years constructing fabs and data centres across Ireland, Europe, the USA and Asia
  • Resident in the State, leading the advanced programmes in person, not by video link
  • Auditing every centre, so a trainee in a district ITI meets the same standard as one at the hub
06 For government partners A partnership

What we bring, and what we ask.

FTR brings the faculty, the curriculum, the international standard and the delivery discipline. We ask the State to bring the institutional platform, the recognition and the mission support that only it can provide.

  1. 01

    Certification recognition

    NSQF / NCVET alignment, so a trainee is certified for deployment in India and abroad.

  2. 02

    Designation within the mission

    Recognition as a designated M&E construction and commissioning skilling partner.

  3. 03

    Access to the training network

    Government training institutions designated as Centres of Excellence and upgraded to one specification.

  4. 04

    Industry linkage

    On-the-job training and offtake discussions with anchor fab, ATMP and data-centre projects.

  5. 05

    Support under existing schemes

    Per-candidate support routed through established State and Central skilling schemes, so no cost falls on the trainee.

  6. 06

    Administrative help

    Single-window support for approvals, utilities, faculty visas, hostel and stipend provision.

What stays

The capability stays permanently with the State — built into Government institutions, delivered by Government-employed instructors, and reusable on every future project the State attracts.
