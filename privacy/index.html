Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Ftright Tech-Infra & Solutions Private Limited (“FTR”, “First Time Right”), CIN U42909GJ2026PTC181309. For privacy questions, write to info@ftright.com.

What we collect

Only what you give us through our forms: your name, contact details, organisation, role, country, the content of your message and, for careers enquiries, details such as qualification, trade, experience and a CV you choose to upload.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, to assess job, trainer and skill development applications and enquiries, and to run our business. We do not sell personal data and we do not add you to a mailing list because you wrote to us.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered to FTR by a third-party form service, Web3Forms, and read by FTR staff. Our email runs on Google Workspace. These providers act on our instructions.

Cookies and analytics

Analytics cookies are set only after you consent through the cookie banner. If you decline, none are set. Strictly necessary storage, such as remembering your choice, does not need consent.

Your rights in India

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. We handle personal data in line with the Information Technology Act 2000 and its rules.

Your rights if you are in the EU or UK

Under the GDPR you also have the right to object to or restrict processing and to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Our legal bases are your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.

Keeping data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was given, or as the law requires, and then delete it.

Exercising your rights

Email info@ftright.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
