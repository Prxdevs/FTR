MEP & Cleanroom Skills Development

India’s first structured M&E construction and commissioning certification programme.

India is building chip designers and fab operators. FTR trains the specialist M&E trades who physically build those facilities — piping, electrical, HVAC, cleanroom fit-out and commissioning — to the standard semiconductor and data centre projects demand.
