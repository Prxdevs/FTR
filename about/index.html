About FTR

Irish and European mission-critical know-how, skilled and built into India’s next generation of facilities.

FTR (First Time Right) is an India-based mission-critical M&E construction, consultancy and skills company.

01 Who we are

What FTR does.

FTR (First Time Right) is an India-based mission-critical M&E construction, consultancy and skills company. We work on semiconductor fabs, ATMP/OSAT units, hyperscale data centres and cleanroom facilities, and help European companies establish their base in India.

  • Semiconductor fabs
  • ATMP/OSAT units
  • Hyperscale data centres
  • Cleanroom facilities
Our mission

Build India’s mission-critical facilities right first time — and leave the skills behind so the capability stays in India.

02 Our approach

Three commitments in how we work.

  • Irish and European execution standards, applied to the Indian programme

  • Commissioning and quality planned from day one, not bolted on at the end

  • Train-the-trainer model: every project and programme builds Indian capability

Our observations relate to the mission-critical facility class, not to Indian construction generally.

03 Leadership Who you will deal with

The people behind FTR.

  • Executive Director, Corporate Affairs

    Vishnubhai (ND) Chaudhari

    Executive Director, Corporate Affairs

    ND (Vishnubhai) Chaudhari is Executive Director, Corporate Affairs at FTR. He brings more than 35 years of experience across Indian industry, real estate development and enterprise leadership. He has developed and built his own commercial centres, giving him first-hand command of project planning, approvals and construction delivery in the Indian market.

    Over three decades, he has built long-standing, trusted relationships with senior leaders across the public sector and Indian industry. These relationships let FTR align its programmes with national and state priorities, and engage partners with credibility from the outset.

    At FTR, he guides market strategy, institutional engagement and the company’s long-term vision as India expands its semiconductor, data centre and critical infrastructure base.
