We build and commission the cleanrooms, ultra-pure water systems and power behind India’s chip plants — and prove they work before production starts.

01 Why it’s hard The challenge

One speck of dust can ruin a batch.

A chip plant is built to cleanliness standards far beyond any ordinary building. Air, water and gases must hold to specification all the time, because a small fault in the building can wipe out a whole batch of wafers. That is why these plants are accepted on how they perform, not on whether they look finished.

The standards are exact: air filtered through A very fine air filter that traps almost every airborne particle. It is why a cleanroom stays clean. filters, the right pressure held between rooms (Keeping the cleaner room at slightly higher air pressure, so dirty air can never drift in.), static control (Electrostatic discharge: a tiny static spark that can destroy a microchip.) and cleanroom construction graded to The international standard that grades how clean the air in a cleanroom has to be..

Where the standard comes from

Taiwan’s PSMC, Tata’s own partner at Dholera, treats zero contamination as standard practice. Ireland’s Intel Leixlip campus, a €17 billion investment, shows what thirty years of cleanroom know-how looks like. FTR’s leadership came up in that Irish cluster.

02 What we deliver Scope

What we deliver.

Five things, delivered by one team to one standard.

  • Clean utilities

    Ultra-pure (Deionised water: water stripped of minerals so nothing in it can contaminate a chip.) water, specialty gases and process piping, built and proven to specification. They touch the product directly.

  • Power

    Low- and medium-voltage distribution: switchboards, transformers, busbar and earthing. A fab cannot lose power or tolerate poor earthing.

  • Cleanroom discipline

    Gowning, contamination control, static control and pressure control between rooms, kept up by every trade on site.

  • Trade coordination

    Every trade on a fab floor works in the same tight space. We sequence them so no one undoes anyone else’s work.

  • Inspection and commissioning

    Written inspection and sign-off at every stage, to the same rigour as our pharma work, then Proving a finished facility actually works as designed before it is handed over. through to handover.
